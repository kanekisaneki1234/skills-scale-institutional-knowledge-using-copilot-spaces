# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Manager

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers identify, analyze, and manage stakeholder interests and expectations throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure that diverse stakeholder concerns are understood, integrated into project planning, and communicated transparently.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder analysis and mapping to identify all project influencers
- Develop and maintain stakeholder engagement plans
- Facilitate regular stakeholder updates and feedback sessions
- Identify and mitigate stakeholder conflicts or concerns
- Ensure stakeholder requirements are incorporated into project scope
- Coordinate communication between project team and key stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Works closely to incorporate stakeholder feedback into project plans and identify stakeholder-related risks
- **Product Manager**: Collaborates to ensure stakeholder needs align with product vision and prioritization
- **Developers**: Communicates stakeholder requirements and provides context on business impact

### Goals
- Maximize stakeholder satisfaction and buy-in
- Reduce scope creep and stakeholder-driven rework
- Build trust and maintain transparent communication with all parties

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and surveys
- Engagement plans and communication matrices
- Regular stakeholder briefings and feedback sessions

---

## Risk Owner

### Role Summary
Risk Owners are dedicated leaders responsible for identifying, monitoring, and managing project risks throughout the project lifecycle. They implement mitigation strategies and ensure risks are escalated appropriately to enable proactive decision-making.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate risk identification workshops and ongoing risk discovery
- Maintain and update the risk register with identified risks, probability, and impact
- Monitor risk indicators and trigger conditions
- Develop and execute mitigation and contingency plans
- Report on risk status and escalate critical risks
- Coordinate risk response activities across the team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Partners to incorporate risk management into project planning and escalate critical risks
- **Developers**: Works with technical teams to assess technical risks and develop mitigation strategies
- **Stakeholder Manager**: Collaborates on stakeholder-related risks and communication implications

### Goals
- Prevent or minimize project disruptions from identified risks
- Enable data-driven risk responses and trade-off decisions
- Maintain a culture of proactive risk awareness

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessment reports
- Risk mitigation plans and status updates
- Risk escalation notifications

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and enforce quality standards, oversee testing strategies, and ensure that deliverables meet specified requirements and quality criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Develop and maintain comprehensive test plans and testing strategies
- Oversee test execution and manage defect tracking
- Ensure adequate test coverage and validation of deliverables
- Collaborate on quality metrics and continuous improvement initiatives
- Coordinate between development and stakeholders on quality issues

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Works collaboratively to establish testable acceptance criteria, review code quality, and manage defects
- **Project Manager**: Provides quality status updates and works to resolve quality-related schedule impacts
- **Product Manager**: Validates that deliverables meet product requirements and user expectations

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet defined quality standards
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Maintain stakeholder confidence in solution quality

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Quality status reports and metrics
- Defect reports and resolution tracking

---

## Change Management Lead

### Role Summary
Change Management Leads guide the organizational adoption of project outcomes and changes. They develop strategies to minimize disruption, support teams through transitions, and ensure successful implementation of new processes or systems.

### Responsibilities
- Assess organizational readiness and impact of project changes
- Develop comprehensive change management and communication plans
- Coordinate training and support programs for end users
- Manage change resistance and address stakeholder concerns
- Monitor adoption metrics and provide post-implementation support
- Align change communication with broader organizational initiatives

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Manager**: Partners on timeline and scope to ensure adequate change management activities
- **Stakeholder Manager**: Collaborates to identify stakeholder impacts and concerns related to organizational change
- **Product Manager**: Works to communicate product benefits and adoption strategies to users

### Goals
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction with project outcomes
- Minimize disruption and productivity loss during transitions
- Build organizational capability for managing ongoing change

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and readiness reports
- Communication plans and change tracking updates
- Training materials and end-user support documentation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide strategic technical guidance on system design, technology decisions, and implementation approaches. They ensure that solutions are technically feasible, scalable, and aligned with organizational standards and long-term vision.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate technology options and recommend solutions aligned with organizational standards
- Design system architecture and technical approaches
- Review technical plans for feasibility and alignment with standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Provide guidance on performance, scalability, and maintainability
- Support cross-team alignment on technical decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides technical design guidance, reviews implementation approach, and supports problem-solving
- **Project Manager**: Identifies technical risks and timeline implications; coordinates with other technical dependencies
- **Risk Owner**: Works together to identify and mitigate technical and architectural risks

### Goals
- Enable technically sound and sustainable solutions
- Reduce rework from architectural misalignments or poor technology choices
- Maintain system quality, scalability, and maintainability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture diagrams
- Technology evaluation and recommendation reports
- Technical review meetings and architectural decision logs
